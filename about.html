Who I am

About

I'm a Computer Science engineer who ended up building AI agents almost by accident — I went in curious about data, and stayed for the problem of getting a machine to hold up its end of a conversation reliably, at scale, for people who have no patience for it failing.

Right now that means building at Avaamo, an enterprise conversational AI company: agents that verify identity, schedule patient visits, and answer IT and HR questions for real employees and real patients, live in production. Before that, at 5C Network — an AI-native radiology platform reading scans for thousands of hospitals — I worked on the workflows and reporting infrastructure that let clinicians actually trust what the system was telling them.

The thread through all of it is the same: I like systems where AI and humans have to work together and neither one can fake it. That means I end up moving across the stack a lot — agent logic, SQL, data pipelines, the product decisions in between — instead of staying in one lane. I'd rather understand how the whole thing holds together than own one narrow piece of it.

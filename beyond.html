Beyond work

I've had a racket in my hand since I was a kid — lawn tennis was my first love, and these days I split my time between table tennis, badminton and pickleball, usually whichever one I can rope someone into playing with me that week. There's something about a sport where you're constantly reacting to another person that I never get tired of.

Outside of that, I'm always up for dancing, always planning the next trip, and always, always hungry — I'd genuinely call myself a foodie before almost anything else. And if you get me talking, I'll probably end up asking about your life instead of talking about mine. I like meeting new people and hearing how they got to where they are; it's the same curiosity that got me into building things, just pointed at people instead of problems.
